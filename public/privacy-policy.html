FuelFire privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This page describes the behavior implemented in the current app. It does not promise cloud sync, provider retention periods, or any feature that the app has not implemented.

Data kept on this device

Profile details, completed workouts, food logs, saved plans, health summaries, settings, and unfinished workout or food drafts are stored in the app's device-local browser storage. A fresh install has no fabricated account history. The account page can export an allow-listed JSON backup and validate an import before changing local data.

The backup contains only FuelFire-owned data keys. It excludes credentials, access and session tokens, RevenueCat identifiers and entitlement cache, subscription-event records, and unrelated origin storage. Provider and authentication caches that are outside the allowlist remain on the device and are not deleted by the local-data action. Import supports an explicit merge or replace choice; canceling or rejecting a file does not erase local data. The app does not claim iCloud synchronization or automatic cross-device restore.

Features that use remote services

Local logging and viewing do not require a remote service. When you choose an AI feature, the relevant request can travel through a FuelFire server. Depending on the server configuration and feature, that request can be routed to Claude or Qwen for text or image analysis. Food photos, treadmill-console photos, typed food descriptions, and voice-derived text are sent only when you invoke the feature that needs them.

Server operators and AI providers may process a request to return a result. Retention, residency, and provider handling can depend on the active deployment and provider terms, so FuelFire does not make a blanket no-retention promise here. Review the applicable provider information before sending sensitive content.

Barcode lookup may also contact the Open Food Facts service. App Store purchase and entitlement operations use the installed RevenueCat/App Store bridge when it is available. The account page reports only the entitlement status exposed by that bridge; it does not invent a plan, renewal date, purchase history, or payment method.

Health and device permissions

If you enable a native health connection, the app can read the categories allowed by the operating-system permission and may cache the resulting summary locally for the health screens. You control those permissions in the operating system. Health data is not included in a remote request unless you choose a feature that sends relevant information.

Diagnostics

Some enabled AI failure paths send a small, allow-listed technical diagnostic to the FuelFire server. Automatic diagnostics use bounded error codes, stages, counts, app/device metadata, and timing fields rather than food details or media. A user-initiated voice diagnostic report can include the transcript or alternatives needed to investigate that report. The diagnostic endpoint excludes raw audio, photos, authorization headers, account identifiers, and precise location.

Your choices

  • Use local features without invoking an AI or health integration.
  • Review, export, import, or delete FuelFire-owned local data from My account.
  • Manage or restore App Store purchases through the real App Store subscription page and the installed purchase bridge.
  • Use operating-system settings to revoke health or other native permissions.

Deleting local app data does not cancel a subscription and does not remove RevenueCat or installation credentials. Those are intentionally outside the local-data deletion scope.

Sharing and security

FuelFire does not sell local app records. Data is shared with a remote service only when needed for a feature you invoke, for the App Store/RevenueCat purchase flow, or for an enabled diagnostic path described above. Local storage and network transport can still be exposed by a compromised device, browser, account, provider, or deployment; no electronic storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, use the support or contact channel provided with the FuelFire distribution you installed. This policy may be updated when the app's storage, providers, or diagnostic behavior changes. The date above identifies the version shown in this build.
