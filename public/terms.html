FuelFire feature terms

Informational feature summary, updated October 28, 2026

This page explains current feature behavior. It is informational and does not add pricing, subscription promises, or a new contract.

Local fitness and food features

You can record workouts, food, profile details, saved plans, health summaries, and unfinished drafts on the device. The app's account page provides a real local JSON export and a validated merge or replace import. Keep a backup before replacing local records.

AI-assisted features

AI food, meal-plan, workout, treadmill, and related features are optional and depend on the installed build, network, access status, and configured service. AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Review and correct results before relying on them or saving them as your own record. Local logging remains a separate path where available.

Health permissions

Health integrations use operating-system permissions. You choose whether to connect them and which permissions to grant. The app does not treat a denied or unavailable permission as proof of a health value.

Purchases and entitlements

When an App Store purchase flow is enabled in the installed build, Apple and the installed RevenueCat bridge handle purchase and entitlement state. Use the App Store subscription page to manage an existing subscription and use the account page's restore action when available. This page does not state a price, renewal date, cancellation policy, or entitlement that the app cannot verify.

Data and feature limits

Device loss, storage clearing, an unavailable network, provider errors, permission changes, or an app update can affect access to a feature or locally stored data. See the privacy policy for the current local-storage, remote-provider, and diagnostic disclosures. Use My account to review local data actions.
